Secret Wards

Offline privacy firewall for multilingual Indian healthcare exports | Track 3 | riddhimajaiswal18/Secret-Wards

Problem and approach

Healthcare exports combine structured records, clinical notes, English, Devanagari and Hinglish identity clues, and medically important numbers. Secret Wards uses a deterministic Python pipeline to parse supported files into a common locator-aware representation, detect selected direct identifiers, preserve clinical spans, apply explicit edits and quarantine inputs that cannot be safely parsed. It is offline and uses no LLM or runtime model download.

Architecture and implementation

StageHow it works
Ingestion and IRRoutes CSV/TSV, JSON/FHIR-shaped JSON, XML and text through bounded parsers; retains source locators and annotations; quarantines known parse failures. PDF/image processing depends on optional local tools.
Detection and understandingUses field/key/header semantics, structural context, deterministic identifier patterns and contextual name/relative rules. Clinical protection helps retain diagnoses, medicines and measurements.
Identity and privacyHMAC tokens use direct identifiers or exact patient-name/phone composites with sex/DOB conflict checks. Supported paths apply date shifts, age bands, locality generalization and PIN masking. Rare-combination suppression is not implemented.
Output and securityWrites sanitized files, SHA-256 report mapping and records manifest; avoids raw-copy fallback. Secret is environment-provided; an independent blocking release gate and encryption of persisted intermediates remain incomplete.

Known limitations and next work

Native pdftoppm and Tesseract executables were absent, so image-only PDF rendering and pixel OCR were unavailable. Broad alias and homonym resolution, full FHIR R4 validation, rare-combination suppression and attack-based re-identification measurement remain incomplete. The verifier is separate from the runtime pipeline. Results are synthetic development-set evidence, not hidden-set accuracy or a privacy guarantee. Do not use real patient data or claim anonymity.
